## SECTION 1: PROJECT CONTEXT

### WHAT IT IS
Kung Fu Legacy is a selective-entry Wing Chun brotherhood and internal school.
NOT a gym. NOT a coaching program. NOT a class-based martial arts school.
Closest analogues: Freemasonry, a fraternal order, a guild.
Wing Chun is the discipline structure that binds the brotherhood together.
3-year goal: a small group of structured, reliable men who embody the system —
some prepared to carry the lineage forward.

### LINEAGE
Yip Man → Moy Yat → Moy Don (Thornton Williams) → Rafael Gonzalez (Moy Don Xùn)
Structure name: Moy Yat Moy Don Ving Tsun Special Student Association

### THE MODEL
- 1-on-1 weekly or bi-weekly mentorship + weekly group session
- Maximum fifteen men at a time
- In-person only. Converse, Texas (primary). San Juan, Puerto Rico (secondary).
- This is not remote. This is not a course.
- Progression-based: Siu Nim Tau → Chum Kiu → Biu Jee → Muk Yan Jong → Long Pole → Double Knives
- Hong bao (offering) at each progression milestone:
    Siu Nim Tau: $100 / Chum Kiu: $200 / Biu Jee: $300
    Muk Yan Jong: $400 / Long Pole: $500 / Double Knives: $600
    Total progression path: $2,100
- Bai Si ceremony (indoor disciple initiation): $3,000
    Earned through trust, not purchasable. Rafael decides, not the student.
- Monthly membership: $1,000–$1,500 (Texas) / $400–$800 (Puerto Rico)

### THE IDEAL MEMBER
NOT broken men. NOT martial arts hobbyists.
- Deep, profound, serious thinker
- Already capable and smart — seeks elevation, not rescue
- Hungry for real brotherhood with men of the same caliber
- Passionate about kung fu, fitness, nutrition, business, entrepreneurship
- Willing to contribute his skills to the collective good
- The kind of man you trust with your back

Disqualifiers: bad energy, resistance to structure, unwilling to contribute to the collective.

### COPY PRINCIPLE — NON-NEGOTIABLE
Rafael is the custodian and gatekeeper of a lineage.
He is NEVER positioned as a lifestyle guru, status superior, or life coach.
His domain authority is Wing Chun and the Moy Yat lineage ONLY.
All copy must stay inside that boundary.

### IMAGE PRINCIPLE — NON-NEGOTIABLE
All imagery must communicate: seriousness, authority, humility, ritual, training, transmission.
Lifestyle branding, influencer aesthetics, fashion/editorial coolness, and personality-forward
imagery are incompatible with this positioning and must not appear on the site.
Sunglasses portraits have been explicitly removed and must not return.

### DESIGN SYSTEM
Dark luxury aesthetic.
Colors: #0B0B0B (matte-black), #2B1F1A (espresso), #C6A962 (gold), #E8E3D9 (bone)
Typography: serif (Playfair) for emotional/voice lines, sans (Inter) for labels/functional text
Gold used for emphasis. Italics for voice/tone lines.
