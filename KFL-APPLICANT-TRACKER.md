---

## SCORING GUIDE

**Application Quality Score (1–5)**
- 5 — Specific, serious, self-aware. Answered all three questions with depth.
- 4 — Strong on 2 of 3. Minor vagueness in one answer.
- 3 — Generic on purpose questions. Adequate on facts. Needs a call to assess.
- 2 — Vague, surface-level, or inconsistent across answers.
- 1 — Disqualifying language, wrong expectations, or bad energy on the page.

**Fit Classification**
- Clear Fit — Language, background, and purpose align without ambiguity.
- Unclear Fit — Promising signals but gaps that require a call to resolve.
- Poor Fit — Misaligned expectations, wrong reasons, or disqualifying indicators.

**Price-Qualified**
- Yes — Profession and income signals support the commitment.
- Unclear — Insufficient information to assess.
- No — Clear signals the number would be a barrier.

**Brotherhood Concept — Do they get it?**
- Yes — Describes it as a lineage, a brotherhood, or something earned. Not a gym, not a course.
- Partial — Understands the seriousness but frames it around technique or self-improvement.
- No — Describes it as a class, a program, or uses language that signals a consumer mindset.
