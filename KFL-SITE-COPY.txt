================================================================================
WHAT THIS SITE IS
================================================================================

Kung Fu Legacy is a selective Wing Chun mentorship led by Rafael Gonzalez — Sifu,
lineage holder under Ip Man → Moy Yat → Thornton Williams (Moy Don) → Rafael Gonzalez
(Moy Don Xùn). This is not a school. It is a private brotherhood of men, capped at 15.
Entry is by application and personal review only.

The site has one job: qualify the right man and get him to submit an application.
Wrong men should self-select out. Some readers should bounce. The copy is premium,
restrained, and selective. It does not sell. It identifies.
